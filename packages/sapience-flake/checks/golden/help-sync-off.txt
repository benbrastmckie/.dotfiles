sapience review [--all]       review pending notes and sync approved notes
sapience pending              how many new findings are waiting
sapience sync                 extract from new work now (stages for review)
sapience coverage             see which work was read or skipped, and why
sapience backfill [days]      catch up over a wide window (default 365)
sapience auto-approve on|off  sync new notes without review
sapience projects             choose which projects sync and require review
sapience reset                fresh local start (hosted Sapience untouched)
sapience stats                see how your Sapience is helping you
sapience status               per-surface token check (terminal / Claude Code / Codex)
sapience adddoc <file>        add a PDF/.txt/.md to your Sapience
sapience adddir <dir>         add a directory of papers and notes
sapience share <project> --with <name>   sync a workstream with a collaborator
sapience join <channel-id> <project>     join a workstream shared with you
sapience shares               list your sync channels
sapience unshare <channel-id> leave a channel (pauses it for both sides)
sapience recall <channel-id>  withdraw what you shared through a channel
sapience company              your team workspace membership
sapience update               fetch the latest client files from the vendor
sf1 ["question"]              chat with your Sapience in the terminal
