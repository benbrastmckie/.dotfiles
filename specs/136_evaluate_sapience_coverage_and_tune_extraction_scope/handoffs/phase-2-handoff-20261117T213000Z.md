## What NOT to Try

- Do NOT poll with `systemctl --user is-active --quiet sapience-sync.service` to detect
  completion of this unit -- it will return a false "inactive/not-active" reading almost
  immediately because the unit's running state is `activating`, not `active`. Poll
  `systemctl --user show sapience-sync.service -p ActiveState --value` instead, and loop while
  the value is `activating` or `active`; stop once it settles to anything else (`inactive` on a
  clean finish).
- Do NOT treat a flat or non-dropping `would ingest now` reading as evidence the pass failed to
  do anything -- Pass 2's own journal confirms 539 objects were genuinely staged even though the
  counter held flat, because new transcripts accrued concurrently. The counter alone is not a
  drain-rate proxy without a same-pass before/after pair plus knowledge of concurrent accrual.
- Do NOT run phase 3 (or any later pass) less than 24 hours after Pass 2's timestamp
  (2026-11-17T21:23:08Z) -- the plan's own spacing requirement, not a formality.
